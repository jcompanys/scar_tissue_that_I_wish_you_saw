\section{Transmurality Aggregation Levels}
\label{app:transmuralagg}

Bin-level onset and transmurality values (Equations~\ref{eq:depthfrac}--\ref{eq:transmurality})
are aggregated over scar-positive bins at three levels.
\textbf{Segment:} mean over scar-positive bins within each AHA segment's polar boundaries (17-element per-patient vector).
\textbf{Territory:} same aggregation pooled across a territory's constituent segments (LAD, RCA, or LCX).
\textbf{Fragment:} each 3D connected component's polar footprint, giving per-scar-island wall-depth penetration.
